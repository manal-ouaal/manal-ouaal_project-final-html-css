Online Violence & Cyber Harassment

Behind every screen, there is a real person. Online violence is real violence. Every woman and girl deserves to feel safe, respected, and heard - online and offline.

Every 25 November, the world unites in orange to demand an end to gender- based violence - standing with every woman silenced, threatened, or harassed across digital spaces.

Understanding Online Violence

Online violence and cyber harassment encompass any gender-based abuse committed, assisted, or aggravated through digital technologies, phones, and social platforms.

Persistent insults, coordinated dogpiling, humiliation, and targeted hostility meant to demean, isolate, or drive women offline.

Distributing, threatening to leak, or digitally altering intimate or private photos and videos without explicit permission.

Direct messages, emails, or comments containing threats of physical harm, sexual violence, blackmail, or intimidation.

Tracking location, monitoring online activity, accessing private accounts, or publishing personal addresses (doxxing).

Creating fraudulent profiles using a woman's name, photos, or identity to damage her reputation or deceive others.

Sending unsolicited explicit images, unwelcome sexual remarks, or coercive requests across messaging and social platforms.
