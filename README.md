HopeVox

(Your Voice Matters. You Are Not Alone.)

1. About the Project

HopeVox is a website created to raise awareness about online violence and cyber harassment against women and girls.

The goal of this project is to provide information, share safety tips, and guide women to organizations where they can find support.

2. Website Pages

page 1 :Home

The Home page introduces online violence and cyber harassment.

It includes:

- Hero Section: A short introduction with images that change on hover.
- section2:  Understanding Online Violence: Visual explanations using images .
- section3: Six Supportive Steps: Six steps to help and support victims.
- section4: Support Organizations: Information about organizations that can help women:
  - UFA
  - ADFM
  - ONU
  - Ministere de la Solidarite, de l'Insertion Sociale et de la Famille

  page2:    Safety Check

This page focuses on online image safety and digital protection.

It includes:

- Hero Section: A background image with an introduction.
- section 2: Reverse Image & Safety Check: A photo upload area with demo results.
- section 3: Quick Safety Actions: Four simple steps to improve online safety.
- section 4: Check Your Information Online: A form with checkboxes to help users review their online information.

page 3: Contact

The Contact page allows visitors to share their messages.

It includes:

Hero Section: A background image with the message "Your Voice Matters".

Contact Form: Input fields created with DaisyUI and change it  with tailwend.
